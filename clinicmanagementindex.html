❓ How to use this website

  1. Sign in – type your username and password, then press the blue Sign In button.
  2. Find a doctor – press Find Doctor. Choose a hospital, then press Book next to the doctor.
  3. Book a visit – write the patient name, choose the date and time, then press Save Appointment.
  4. Hear the page – press Read Aloud and the page will be read to you.
  5. Bigger writing – press Large Text to turn big writing on or off.
  6. Go back to the start – press Dashboard in the menu.
